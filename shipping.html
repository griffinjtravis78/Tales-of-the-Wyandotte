Shipping & Returns Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 | Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Shipping & Fulfillment

We utilize a hybrid fulfillment model to bring our books, companion audio series, and heritage lifestyle goods to you:

  • Books & Amazon Orders: Physical books fulfilled directly via Amazon or partner distributors adhere to standard Amazon shipping speeds and policies.
  • Print-on-Demand (POD) Apparel & Goods: Custom apparel and lifestyle merchandise are manufactured and shipped via third-party partners (such as Printify). Production typically takes 2 to 7 business days, followed by standard carrier transit times.
  • Digital Downloads: Digital audio files and companion tracks are delivered instantly via secure download links upon completed checkout.

2. Returns & Refunds

  • Print-on-Demand Items: Because custom apparel and merchandise are printed specifically for your order, we cannot accept returns or issue refunds for buyer’s remorse, incorrect sizing ordered, or change of mind. However, if an item arrives damaged, defective, or misprinted, please contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos of the issue for a replacement or refund.
  • Digital Downloads: Due to the intangible nature of digital audio files and e-books, all sales of digital products are final and non-refundable once accessed or downloaded.

3. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding your order status, shipping times, or need assistance with a damaged item, please contact us at contact@talesofthewyandotte.us.
