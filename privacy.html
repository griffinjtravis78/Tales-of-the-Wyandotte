Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 | Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Welcome to Tales of the Wyandotte (the "Website," "we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, purchase our books, audio companion series, apparel, or related heritage goods.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, shipping address, and phone number provided when you place an order, download digital files, or submit an inquiry.
  • Transaction Data: Details regarding purchases or payments processed securely through our third-party checkout providers (such as Stripe or Gumroad). Note: We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device details, and pages visited via cookies and web analytics tools to help us improve site functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for legitimate business operations, including:

  • Fulfilling orders, processing payments, and coordinating shipping with third-party partners (such as Printify).
  • Responding to customer service inquiries, school/library visit requests, or tribal communications.
  • Sending transactional emails, digital download links, or updates regarding our book series.

3. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with trusted service providers necessary to run our business operations (such as payment gateways, analytics providers, and print-on-demand fulfillment partners), all of whom are obligated to keep your information secure.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at contact@talesofthewyandotte.us.
